Which clues put these 21 domains on the list?

Each domain's score adds four clues. Move a slider from 0 to 1 and the other three adjust in proportion so the four weights always total 1. The list is re-ranked and its top 21 is compared with the original 21. A switch sets a clue to 0 or back to its original weight. Data: 438 domains seen on at least 10 of the 560 infected machines.
